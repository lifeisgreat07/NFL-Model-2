Sports picks, made before the game and graded in public

Two models give every NFL, NHL and NBA game a chance to win before it starts. Each pick is saved where anyone can check it, then graded against the result and the betting market.

A free, open personal project: no tips for sale and no betting advice, and every number comes from code anyone can read.

Times are shown in your time zone.

How it works

  1. 1Picked before the gameTwo models give every game a chance to win. Model A reads how the teams play; Model B adds the betting market.
  2. 2Locked in publicEach pick is saved to the project's public history before kickoff, puck drop or tip-off, so nobody can change it after.
  3. 3Graded afterThe final score grades every pick, and each model is compared with the market.

New here?

What is a model?

A set of rules, fitted to past seasons, that turns how two teams have played into a chance to win.

What is the market?

The betting odds, with the bookmaker's margin taken out. It is hard to beat, and the site says when the models do not.

Can I check the picks?

Yes. Every pick is saved to the project's public history on GitHub before the game starts, and that history shows when each one was saved.
