# Reading Sportalytics

Where to start, by what you want to know. The [README](../README.md) is the
ten-minute version.

## Is it any good?

- [The live site](https://lifeisgreat07.github.io/NFL-Model-2/): each sport's
  picks, record and track record against the market and the base rate.
- The README's [Results at a glance](../README.md#results-at-a-glance): every
  sport's backtest and live log loss in one table, each cell tied to its file
  by a test.
- Each sport's Methodology and Model Lab pages on the site: how the models
  work, and every registered experiment, accepted or rejected.

## How is it built?

- [architecture.md](architecture.md): the whole system on one diagram.
- [decisions/](decisions/README.md): the decisions the rest rests on, one page
  each, with what each costs and what would reopen it.
- [operations.md](operations.md): running it, the scheduled jobs, the
  settings, and what to do when an alert issue opens.

## How is the work checked?

- [VERIFICATION.md](../VERIFICATION.md): no claim without evidence that was
  run.
- [BOOTH_PROTOCOL.md](../BOOTH_PROTOCOL.md), [SPOTTER_PROTOCOL.md](../SPOTTER_PROTOCOL.md)
  and [LINE_JUDGE_PROTOCOL.md](../LINE_JUDGE_PROTOCOL.md): the checking
  agents' rules.
- [case-studies/](case-studies/README.md): real problems, how they were found
  and what they cost, and [lessons-learned.md](lessons-learned.md), what they
  taught.

## What changed, and when?

- [CHANGELOG.md](../CHANGELOG.md): month by month, each piece of planned work
  with its pull requests.
- [stage-history.md](stage-history.md): every stage's full plan, under its
  number.

## Working on it

- [CONTRIBUTING.md](../CONTRIBUTING.md) and [SECURITY.md](../SECURITY.md).
- [context.md](context.md), [index.md](index.md) and [traps.md](traps.md): the
  notes a working session reads first.
