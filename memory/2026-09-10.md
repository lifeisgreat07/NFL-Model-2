# 2026-09-10 — Stage 8 design phase, closed

No code changed. `src/` and `tests/` untouched; the suite is still 704/1.

## What happened

Four rounds with Claude Fable produced the visual direction, v3.0 through v3.4.
The decision record is `docs/design/STAGE8-DESIGN.md`; the specification is the
two mock files beside it. Everything below is why, not what — the what is in
that file.

## The thing that made this work

Every numeric claim was re-derived before being accepted, never read back.
Contrast by recomputing WCAG relative luminance from the hex values in the
token block, with sRGB alpha compositing for tints. Colour-blind separation by
running the Machado 2009 matrices at severity 1.0 and computing CIEDE2000.
Geometry by rendering in headless Chromium and reading `getBoundingClientRect`.

That caught, in order:

1. **v3.0** claimed a contrast pairing at 4.5:1. It computed to 4.22:1.
2. **v3.2** claimed a breakdown row fit on one line at card width. It measured
   65px against 48px for its neighbours — three lines. The cause was not the
   label but the column allocation: printing the subject inside the value cell
   had widened that column under `table-layout: auto`, and two other rows that
   previously fit began wrapping too.
3. **v3.3** claimed a colour-blind separation floor of 15 was met. One pair,
   light deuteranopia Model B vs My Picks, computed to 14.7.

By v3.4, 58 of 58 contrast rows and 18 of 18 CVD pair distances matched to the
stated decimal, and all twelve geometry renders measured exactly as claimed.
The improvement came from telling Fable to report the measurement rather than
the adjective, and to mark explicitly what it had not rendered.

**Two of the false claims after that were mine, not Fable's.** Writing the
brand mark, I asserted in a comment that three CSS rules shared a specificity
so source order would decide. They did not: `.brand:hover .brand-mark .spin` is
four classes and my `.is-loading` rule was three, so hovering the logo during a
load froze the spinner — and because the tumble was then never running, the
"wait for the revolution to finish" logic hung and the loading state stuck on
permanently. One wrong assumption, two failures, both found by measuring rather
than by re-reading the code I had just written. Then, adding the `Home` tag, I
assumed home rows had spare width because the 146px measurement came from an
away row. Graded home rows carry a score; they overflowed at 174px.

## Decisions worth remembering the reasoning for

**Cutting beat adding, twice.** Mark called the four stat tiles and the
per-card `A - MARKET` field redundant. Removing `A - MARKET` and restating the
market probability for the same team the models name made the comparison
*more* available, not less — the gap became two numbers side by side instead of
a signed derived figure in different units. Deleting the calculation improved
the page.

**A specification can be wrong in a way the implementer cannot see.** I asked
for the market probability to name "the team the card's top row names". Fable
implemented that exactly. The top row is always the away team, and the team the
eye lands on is the bold favourite, which is often the home team — so on
BAL @ CLE it read `68% BAL` beside Model A's `53% CLE`, forcing mental
subtraction on precisely the cards that matter. My wording, their faithful
work.

**Two encodings were built and rejected, both for stated reasons.** A `PICK`
badge: a sixth signal for a fact already encoded five ways, in the visual
language of a tout sheet, and on an `A != B` card it stamps "PICK" on a row
Model B disagrees with. A perpetually spinning logo: motion in peripheral
vision on every page reads as amateur and undoes the restraint the rest of the
design earns. Both were shown side by side with the alternatives rather than
argued about in prose, which settled each in one exchange.

**Mark could not see the difference between the subtle pick treatments.** That
was the answer, not a failure of the demo: if the accent rail alone does not
register, it is not achieving "louder". Shipped the rail *and* the words.

## Two traps found by rendering, now written into the design record

- An animated disclosure built on `grid-template-rows: 0fr` leaves collapsed
  content in the accessibility tree and in find-in-page, contradicting the
  button's `aria-expanded="false"`. `display: none` had been hiding it
  correctly; the animation quietly removed that.
- Wholesale `innerHTML` re-render destroys interaction state. Fable's own
  architecture note recommended keeping such state in `state` and re-deriving
  it — and then did not do it, in the same document. An open breakdown vanished
  on a filter change.
